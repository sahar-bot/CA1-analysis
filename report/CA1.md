# Property Maintenance Request Service

## Orgranisation


Kingdom Community Property Services - Company


Errors:

- Different staff members use different descriptions for the same room, asset or fault
- Request status is not always current
- The meaning of emergency, urgent and routine is not applied consistently.
- Staff do not consistently distinguish no access, occupant cancellation and contractor delay.
- Staff currently use phrases such as fixed, safe for now, awaiting parts and needs another visit without agreed meanings.
- Some properties have several responsible organisations, and it is not always clear who should approve or pay for work. 

Properties: 

- community buildings
- small offices 
- rented residential properties


Who can make Requests:

- occupants
- caretakers
- property managers
- parter orgranisations


Who completes: 

- internal maintenance staff
- approved external contractors


What information stored:

- requests
- photographs
- quotations
- appointments
- completion notes


Where is information stored:

- email
- spreadsheet
- paper job sheets
- messaging groups


What is inside a request: 

- property
- location
- reported problem
- contact person
- access availability



Request can be:

- accepted
- returned for more information
- referred to another organisation
- considered occupant's responsibility
